Your Peaceful Sanctuary in Kathmandu

Valley View Family Resort offers an unmatched retreat from the hustle and bustle of city life, located on the picturesque slopes of Chandragiri.

Designed with family and peace in mind, our resort combines modern amenities with traditional Nepalese warmth. Whether you are walking through our organic gardens, enjoying an evening campfire with family, or simply sipping local tea while watching the sunset paint the valley below, you will find comfort and renewal at every corner.

Resort Services & Amenities

We host a variety of premium amenities designed to make your family staycation refreshing and enjoyable.

Multi-Cuisine Restaurant

Relish traditional organic local Nepalese cuisine (Dhido, Local Chicken) along with popular continental dishes, fresh from our kitchen.

High-Speed Wi-Fi

Stay connected with high-speed internet covering the entire property. Perfect for remote work while nestled in nature.

Campfire & Barbecue

Gather around the warm stone bonfire under the starry Kathmandu sky. We arrange custom barbecues and live music setups on request.

Secure Free Parking

Spacious, secure, and free on-site parking for all our guests' private cars, SUVs, and motorbikes with 24/7 security.

Guided Nature Trails

Explore the lush forested paths of Chandragiri with local guides. Enjoy morning bird watching and spectacular view points.

Family & Kids Play Area

A safe, grassy playground and indoor board games setup, ensuring that children and families stay entertained during their visit.
